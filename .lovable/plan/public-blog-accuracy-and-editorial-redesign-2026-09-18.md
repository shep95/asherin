# public blog accuracy and editorial redesign

## outcome
refresh every public Asherin article so it reflects only current, publicly disclosable functions and uses the selected radical editorial journal design.

## content audit
- establish one shared article catalogue for titles, summaries, dates, topics, reading times, status, and capability boundaries.
- review every article against current shipped software and project rules.
- replace outdated Aureon-facing names in visible copy and metadata while preserving legacy URLs for existing links.
- remove or qualify unsupported guarantees, private implementation details, provider disclosures, stale product surfaces, and obsolete pricing or workflow claims.
- distinguish current capabilities, archived research notes, conceptual methods, and unavailable functions.
- update the legal-research article first as the reference standard: jurisdiction-aware research, source verification, citation uncertainty, and the explicit boundary that it is not legal advice.

## selected visual direction
- apply the locked paper-contrast palette, Instrument Serif headings, Work Sans body, and radical editorial journal composition.
- rebuild the shared article frame with an asymmetric title, current-status panel, capability-boundary rail, readable editorial body, progress feedback, and restrained motion.
- use the same system across all article pages without flattening their individual diagrams, evidence, or interactive elements.
- improve mobile reading, long-string wrapping, metadata legibility, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion behavior.
- redesign the blog index to match the journal and make current, archived, and conceptual writing easy to distinguish.

## implementation details
- preserve all current public URLs and route compatibility.
- centralize article metadata to prevent the index, page heading, and search metadata from drifting.
- use semantic theme tokens for the selected palette and shared article presentation.
- keep public copy lowercase except where an exact proper noun, acronym, quotation, or legal citation requires otherwise.
- preserve existing structured article, breadcrumb, and FAQ data while correcting their visible claims and metadata.

## verification
- run focused article/catalogue tests, the full type check, and the production build.
- open the blog index and representative product, security, research, pricing, and legal articles on desktop and mobile.
- verify links, filters, status labels, article navigation, overflow, reduced-motion handling, and browser errors.
- compare every listed public article route against the shared catalogue so no article is omitted.
